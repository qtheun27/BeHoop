Terms of use

The service

BeHoop (behoop.be) is a personal, free and ad-free project that shows Belgian and international basketball results and lets you follow clubs and post messages, photos and game videos.

Your account

You must be 13 or older. Your first name and last-name initial are visible to other users. You are responsible for what you post.

What you post

You must have the right to post your photos and videos. Media from a Youth team are only visible to approved members of the Team. You agree to remove content if a parent asks.

Moderation

Any content can be reported. BeHoop may hide content or suspend an account that breaks the charter, without notice.

Liability

Sports data is taken automatically from public sources and may contain errors: when in doubt, the official source prevails. The service is provided "as is", with no guarantee of availability.

Data sources

Results, schedules and standings: AWBB (Wallonia-Brussels), Basketbal Vlaanderen, BNXT League, EuroLeague Basketball (EuroLeague, EuroCup), FIBA (national teams, Champions League, FIBA Europe Cup, European women's competitions), ESPN (NBA, WNBA). News: headlines and short excerpts from the media's public feeds, with a link to the original article. The source is shown under every standings table and schedule.

Trademarks and logos

Names, logos and trademarks of clubs, leagues and competitions belong to their owners. They are only used to identify teams and competitions; BeHoop is not affiliated with or endorsed by any federation, league or club. Flags: flag-icons (MIT licence).

Removal request

A rights holder (federation, club, media outlet, photographer) can ask for content, a logo or a source to be removed by writing to q.theuninck@gmail.com: requests are handled promptly.
