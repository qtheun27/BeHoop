Privacy policy

Data controller

Quentin T. — q.theuninck@gmail.com.

Data collected

Email address and sign-in ID (Firebase Auth), first name and last-name initial, followed clubs and teams, Team memberships, posts, reactions, reports, notification tokens, uploaded photos and videos.

Why

To run your account, your feed, notifications and video editing. No ads, no reselling, no profiling.

Where

Google Firebase (EU/United States, standard contractual clauses) and Cloudflare R2 (media storage). Raw video footage is deleted 48 hours after editing.

YouTube

If you connect your YouTube channel, BeHoop uses the YouTube API Services to publish your match videos to your channel and add them to your playlists, only when you ask. BeHoop keeps your channel's name and ID and an access authorization (stored on our servers, never in the app). By using this feature you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service (youtube.com/t/terms); the Google Privacy Policy applies (policies.google.com/privacy). You can disconnect your channel at any time in your profile (the authorization is then deleted) or revoke access from your Google account (myaccount.google.com/permissions).

Data received from Google

When you sign in with Google, BeHoop receives your email address, name and profile picture, only to create and display your account. BeHoop's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. This data is never sold, used for advertising, or used to train artificial intelligence models.

Audience measurement

To know how many people use BeHoop and which pages are viewed, we use Cloudflare Web Analytics: no cookies, no identifiers, no tracking of a person from one visit to the next. Only aggregate figures (visits, pages, country, device type) are kept.

Retention

As long as your account exists. Content from a deleted account is erased within 30 days (backups included).

Your rights (GDPR)

Access, correction, erasure (“Delete my account” button in Profile), objection, portability. Write to q.theuninck@gmail.com. You can also contact the Data Protection Authority (dataprotectionauthority.be).

Comments and reactions

Your comments are visible to everyone, with your first name and last-name initial; your reactions are counted without your name. You can delete a comment at any time; they are erased with your account.

Minors

Photos and videos of young players are never public and cannot be downloaded. A parent can remove content immediately with “I’m a parent — remove this content”.
