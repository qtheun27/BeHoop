Privacy policy

Data controller

Quentin Theuninck, self-employed (Arch In Tech), company number BE 0597.760.718, rue de Basècles 13, 7321 Bernissart, Belgium — q.theuninck@gmail.com. This is the email address to exercise your rights or ask questions about your data.

In short

BeHoop does no advertising, no data selling and no profiling. Data is only used to run the app. Youth teams' content stays private to the team.

Your account

Email address, login ID, first name and last-name initial, language, notification preferences and your devices' notification tokens. With “Continue with Google”, BeHoop also receives your name and profile photo. Legal basis: performing the service you request (contract).

Your activity

Clubs and teams followed, team memberships, posts, comments, reactions, reports, votes for the play of the game. Legal basis: contract; for reports and safety, the legitimate interest of protecting users.

Club space and Hoop Camp

Role in the club (coach, player, parent, admin), player cards (first name, last name, team; a child can be added by their parent or club, without an account), sessions and drills, Hoop Camp programme, ticked drills, ratings and notes from the parent or coach. This information is only visible to the club members concerned. Legal basis: contract with the parent or player, and the club's legitimate interest in organising its teams.

Carpool

Offers and requests for seats to a game, and your phone number if you give it. The phone number stays private: it is only shown to the people you accept. Legal basis: contract.

Photos and videos

The media you send, edited game videos, Moments, their posters and share cards. Editing automatically analyses the video's sound and movement to suggest highlights: this analysis recognises no one. Original videos (raw footage) are deleted 48 hours after editing. Legal basis: contract; for a minor's image, the parents' consent, confirmed by the person who publishes.

Reading the scoreboard

If you photograph the scoreboard to fill in the score, the photo is sent to Anthropic (Claude) to read the numbers, then not kept by BeHoop. Anthropic does not use it to train its models. Legal basis: your request (contract).

YouTube

If you connect your YouTube channel, BeHoop uses the YouTube API Services to publish your game videos and Moments (as Shorts) on your channel and add them to your playlists, only when you ask, and to check that a published video still exists. BeHoop keeps your channel's name and ID and an access authorisation, stored on our servers, never in the app. By using this feature, you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service (youtube.com/t/terms); the Google Privacy Policy applies (policies.google.com/privacy). You can disconnect your channel at any time in your profile (the authorisation is then deleted) or revoke access from your Google account (myaccount.google.com/permissions). Legal basis: your consent.

Data received from Google

When you sign in with Google, BeHoop receives your email address, name and profile picture, only to create and display your account. BeHoop's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. This data is never sold, used for advertising, or used to train artificial intelligence models.

Music

When you listen to or choose music for a Moment, your device receives it directly from Jamendo (Luxembourg), which therefore sees your IP address. BeHoop does not pass any of your account data to it.

Audience measurement

To know how many people use BeHoop and which pages are viewed, we use Cloudflare Web Analytics: no cookie, no identifier, no tracking of a person from one visit to another. Only aggregate figures (visits, pages, country, device type) are kept. Legal basis: legitimate interest in improving the service.

Minors

You must be 13 to create an account (the age of digital consent in Belgium); a younger child only appears through their parent or club. A youth team's photos and videos are never public: only the team's approved members see them, their share previews show no photo, they cannot be commented on (positive reactions only), and only the person who published them can download them. They can only be published on YouTube as unlisted or private, after confirming the parents' consent. A parent can remove content immediately via “I'm a parent — remove this content”.

Parents' image consent

When a parent joins a team, they state whether their child may appear in the team's photos and videos. They can change their mind at any time on the team page. Team managers see which children must not be shown, and a reminder appears before every photo or video is published. Legal basis: the parent's consent.

Service providers

BeHoop relies on service providers that process data on its behalf, under contract: Google Firebase (login, database, notifications), Cloudflare (server, photo and video storage, statistics), GitHub (video editing, files deleted after each edit), Anthropic (reading the scoreboard). Some are based in the United States: these transfers are covered by the EU–US Data Privacy Framework and/or the European Commission's standard contractual clauses. Google (YouTube) and Jamendo only receive data when you use their features.

Who sees what

Your first name and last-name initial, your public posts and comments are visible to everyone; a private team's content to the team's members; your carpool phone only to the people you accept; your reactions are counted without your name.

Retention

As long as your account exists. A deleted account's content is erased within 30 days, backups included. A deleted video or Moment disappears from the app immediately and its files are erased. Raw footage is deleted 48 hours after editing, the YouTube authorisation as soon as you disconnect your channel. Video-editing technical logs are kept for 90 days at most.

Security

Encrypted connections (HTTPS), access to private content checked by the server on every view, sensitive authorisations (YouTube) kept only on the server. In the event of a data breach presenting a risk, the Data Protection Authority is notified within 72 hours, and the people concerned without delay if the risk is high.

Your rights (GDPR)

Access, rectification, erasure (“Delete my account” button in Profile), restriction, objection, portability, and withdrawing your consent at any time (for example by disconnecting YouTube). Write to q.theuninck@gmail.com: reply within one month. A parent can exercise these rights for their child under 13. You can also lodge a complaint with the Belgian Data Protection Authority, rue de la Presse 35, 1000 Brussels (dataprotectionauthority.be).

Comments and reactions

Your comments are visible to everyone, with your first name and last-name initial; your reactions are counted without your name. You can delete a comment at any time; they are erased with your account.

Updates

This policy describes the app as it works on 27/11/2026. Any new use of your data will be added here before it goes live, and important changes will be announced in the app.
